# About this book

A textbook written as the companion to CMU's Intro to Computer Music Course (15-322). Teaches the principles of computer music theory and practice from a computer science perspective in Python. Written by [Chris Donahue](https://chrisdonahue.com).

:::{important}
**AI Disclaimer**: To allow for a full supporting text to be written on a short summer timeline, this text was written with heavy support from AI. Every word has been thoroughly reviewed by the author (Chris Donahue).
:::
