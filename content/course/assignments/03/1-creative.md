## Direction 1 (Creative): Create a more involved cover of a song on TheoryTab

In assignment 2, you created a simple cover of a song using TheoryTab and Hookpad just by synthesizing the input notes. This direction will ask you to make a more involved cover using Pyquist and TheoryTab, or any other music API/software you'd like. Along with synthesizing the note yourself, here are some other features your cover can have: 

- Realized chords (taking chord chart data and making an instrument play them, either all notes at once, arpeggiated, or some other pattern)
- Multiple instruments (separating bass, melody, accompaniment into different sounds)
- Dynamic Shapes (getting louder and softer, or changing timbre over time)
- Reharmonization (changing the chords of the song to something else, while keeping the melody intact)
- Counterpoint (adding a second melody that harmonizes with the first)

You may also choose to compose your own song instead of covering an existing one. 

### Requirements
- Your song should be at least (45)s long 
- Use at least (3) of the above features in your cover
- All sounds and arranging logic must be generated by your code, though your source of note data is up to you (TheoryTab, Hookpad, MIDI, or your own composition)
- In `CREATIVE.md`, include
  - Title of the song
  - Composer/Songwriter
  - Name exactly which (3) features you chose, briefly describing how you implemented them, and how they occur in the song.
