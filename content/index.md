# CMU Intro to Computer Music

Welcome to _CMU Intro to Computer Music_, the course site and companion text for
15-322/622 at Carnegie Mellon University. It teaches the principles of computer
music theory and practice from a computer science perspective in Python.

- **Course Information**: Resources and assignments for students taking the course.
- **Textbook**: The _Principles of Computer Music_ textbook, written for this course by instructor Chris Donahue.

Have questions or feedback? Contact course staff at `icm-csd@cmu.edu`.
